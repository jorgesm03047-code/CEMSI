MEDICAL DIRECTOR & FOUNDER

Dr. Alejandro Gutiérrez Lazcano

Medical Doctor and Licensed Physical Therapist, certified in Proprioceptive Deep Tendon Reflex (P-DTR®).

He earned his Medical Doctor (Médico Cirujano) degree from the Autonomous University of the State of Hidalgo (UAEH) in 2006. To complement medicine with manual and functional work, he completed the 1,800-hour Chirophysical Diploma at the International School of Health Educators and a Bachelor's degree in Physical Therapy and Rehabilitation at Universidad Privada del Centro, with a degree authenticated by Mexico's Ministry of Education (SEP).

His neurological training includes the Diploma in Neurofacilitation Techniques from UNAM (FES Zaragoza), certification in Neurodynamics and Neuromeningeal Mobilization, and the Foundational and Advanced levels of Proprioceptive Deep Tendon Reflex (P-DTR®). Today he applies this combined expertise at CEMSI to deliver precise diagnoses and non-invasive treatment.

Philosophy of Intervention

The human body is not a machine of isolated parts; it is a complex neurological and structural network. Treating an inflamed tendon without correcting the neurological signal that caused the tension is, medically speaking, a temporary patch. Our philosophy is to resolve the root problem.

01

Causal Treatment

We don't treat symptoms. We evaluate muscle chains and the proprioceptive system to find the exact receptor sending alarm information to the brain.

02

Zero Unnecessary Medication

Painkillers chemically block the pain signal. With P-DTR, we reset the neurological mechanism that generates that signal, making drugs obsolete in many chronic cases.

03

Elite Sports Performance

We apply neurodynamics not only to remove pain, but to maximize motor strength and contraction speed in elite athletes.
